# PrimeCore Minerals

# Information Technology Acceptable Use and Device Policy

## 1. Purpose

PrimeCore Minerals provides permanent staff with a work laptop and work mobile phone to support their duties at the office, at home, and while travelling for approved work purposes. This policy sets expectations for secure, responsible, and fair use of these devices and PrimeCore’s systems, networks, applications, and data.

The policy aims to protect staff, business information, clients, and PrimeCore’s technology environment while allowing reasonable personal use.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent PrimeCore employees issued with a company laptop, mobile phone, SIM/eSIM, or access to PrimeCore systems. It applies whether devices are used at a PrimeCore location, a home office, public location, or while travelling.

## 3. Ownership and Responsibility

All issued laptops, mobile devices, software licences, mobile services, and business data remain the property of PrimeCore Minerals.

Staff must:

* Take reasonable care of issued equipment and keep it secure.
* Use strong passwords, PINs, or biometric protection.
* Report a lost, stolen, damaged, or suspected compromised device immediately.
* Install company-required updates, security software, and configuration changes.
* Return equipment and company information when requested or when employment ends.
* Not allow friends, family members, or unauthorised people to use company devices.

## 4. Work Use

Company devices are provided primarily for legitimate PrimeCore business. Staff must use them in a manner that is professional, secure, lawful, and consistent with workplace policies.

Staff must not use work devices or PrimeCore systems to access, create, store, share, or distribute material that is illegal, discriminatory, threatening, abusive, sexually explicit, offensive, or otherwise inconsistent with a respectful workplace.

## 5. Fair Personal Use

Reasonable personal use is permitted when it:

* Is occasional and does not interfere with work duties or productivity.
* Does not create a security, legal, reputational, or financial risk for PrimeCore.
* Does not exceed mobile-data allowances, generate unreasonable charges, or consume excessive network capacity.
* Does not involve prohibited content or activity.
* Does not breach any PrimeCore workplace policy or applicable law.

Examples of reasonable personal use include checking personal email, limited personal messaging, brief web browsing, online banking, or navigation while travelling.

Personal use must be kept to a sensible level. Staff should use their personal devices or home internet connection for large downloads, streaming, online gaming, or other high-data personal activity.

## 6. Prohibited Use

Staff must not use PrimeCore devices, accounts, networks, or internet services for:

* Illegal downloads, piracy, copyright infringement, or unlicensed software.
* Accessing, downloading, storing, or distributing adult or sexually explicit content.
* Online harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, discrimination, or abusive conduct.
* Gambling, cryptocurrency mining, unauthorised commercial activity, or activities that create excessive data or power use.
* Circumventing security controls, firewalls, web filtering, device management, or monitoring tools.
* Installing unauthorised software, browser extensions, operating systems, VPNs, remote-access tools, or network devices.
* Sharing passwords, multi-factor authentication codes, company accounts, or confidential information.
* Connecting unauthorised storage devices or transferring PrimeCore data to personal cloud storage, personal email, or unauthorised applications.

Any activity that breaches legislation, workplace policy, contractual obligations, or PrimeCore’s Code of Conduct.

7. Security and Remote Work

When working remotely, staff must:

* Use approved secure remote-access methods, including PrimeCore’s approved VPN or secure access service.
* Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive work unless connected through approved secure access.
* Lock devices whenever unattended.
* Keep devices out of sight in vehicles and public places.
* Store company information only in approved PrimeCore systems.
* Be alert for phishing emails, suspicious links, unexpected attachments, and social-engineering attempts.

Staff must not attempt to repair, modify, root, jailbreak, or otherwise alter company devices without approval from PrimeCore IT.

## 8. Data and Privacy

PrimeCore will take reasonable steps to protect employee privacy. However, staff should not expect personal use on company-issued devices or networks to be completely private.

PrimeCore may monitor, log, filter, audit, or investigate the use of company devices, accounts, networks, and data where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, system administration, incident response, or policy enforcement.

## 9. Data Limits and Charges

Staff must use mobile data responsibly. High-data personal activities such as video streaming, large downloads, gaming, or continuous hotspot use may be restricted.

Where usage exceeds an employee’s allocated data limit or causes unreasonable costs, PrimeCore may restrict access, recover costs where appropriate, or review the employee’s device arrangements.

## 10. Reporting Incidents

Staff must report the following to PrimeCore IT or their manager as soon as possible:

* Lost or stolen devices.
* Suspected malware, phishing, or unauthorised account access.
* Accidental disclosure of company or client information.
* Unusual device behaviour, security warnings, or unauthorised software.
* Any suspected breach of this policy.

Prompt reporting will be treated as responsible behaviour and helps reduce harm.

11. Breaches of This Policy

Failure to comply with this policy may result in restricted access, removal of issued equipment, disciplinary action, recovery of unreasonable costs, or other action consistent with PrimeCore workplace procedures and applicable law.
