# PrimeCore Minerals

# Casual Staff BYOD and Limited Network Access Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the conditions under which casual staff may use their own laptops, mobile phones, or tablets to access approved PrimeCore Minerals systems and files.

Casual staff are provided limited access only to the information and services required for their assigned duties. This approach protects PrimeCore’s business, operational, employee, and client information from unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all casual, temporary, contract, and labour-hire staff using a personally owned device while connected to PrimeCore systems, collaboration platforms, internet services, or networks.

It applies whether the casual staff member is working on-site, at home, or remotely.

## 3. Approved Use and Access

Casual staff may access only:

* Their allocated PrimeCore account.
* Shared files, folders, applications, and collaboration spaces specifically provided for their role.
* Approved communication and work platforms required to complete assigned tasks.
* PrimeCore guest or BYOD network services, where access has been authorised.

Access is provided on a need-to-know basis and may be changed, reduced, or removed at any time based on business requirements.

## 4. Restricted Access

Casual staff must not attempt to access or use:

* Network drives, servers, databases, shared folders, or files not specifically assigned to them.
* Other employees’ accounts, files, email, devices, credentials, or collaboration spaces.
* Administrative systems, network-management tools, security systems, or confidential business systems.
* PrimeCore printers, multifunction devices, scanners, or other corporate printing services.
* Internal network resources using unauthorised tools, software, remote-access services, or file-sharing applications.

Casual staff are not permitted to connect their personal device directly to internal corporate network segments. Personal devices must use the designated guest or BYOD network and approved cloud-based collaboration services.

## 5. Shared Files and Information Handling

Casual staff must access and work only with files that have been shared with them for an approved work purpose.

If a casual staff member receives access to a file, folder, email, or other information they do not believe they should be able to see, they must:

* Stop accessing or reading the information.
* Not copy, download, share, print, alter, or discuss the information.
* Report the issue promptly to their PrimeCore supervisor or the IT/security contact.
* Delete any local copy only when directed by PrimeCore IT or their supervisor.

Accidental access must be reported honestly and promptly. Reporting an error helps PrimeCore correct permissions and protect information.

## 6. Device Security Requirements

Before accessing PrimeCore systems, casual staff must ensure their personal device:

* Uses a current supported operating system.
* Has current security updates and anti-malware protection where supported.
* Has a password, PIN, biometric lock, or equivalent screen lock enabled.
* Automatically locks after a reasonable period of inactivity.
* Is not rooted, jailbroken, or deliberately modified to bypass security controls.
* Does not contain known malware or unauthorised access tools.
* Is used only by the authorised casual staff member while accessing PrimeCore information.

PrimeCore may deny or remove access where a device does not meet these minimum security requirements.

7. Data Protection

PrimeCore information must be handled carefully and only for authorised work purposes.

Casual staff must not:

* Save PrimeCore files to personal cloud storage, personal email, USB drives, or unauthorised applications.
* Share PrimeCore information with friends, family, other employers, or unauthorised colleagues.
* Take screenshots, photographs, recordings, or copies of confidential information unless required and authorised for work.
* Download or retain PrimeCore files after their assigned work is complete.
* Use PrimeCore information for personal, commercial, competitive, or unlawful purposes.

Where possible, PrimeCore files must be viewed and edited in approved cloud services so access can be removed when the engagement ends.

## 8. Prohibited Conduct

Casual staff must not use their own devices or PrimeCore network access for:

* Illegal downloads, copyright infringement, or unauthorised software.
* Accessing, storing, or sharing sexually explicit, offensive, discriminatory, abusive, or illegal material.
* Online harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or inappropriate workplace conduct.
* Attempting to bypass access controls, security settings, web filtering, or network restrictions.
* Scanning, probing, intercepting, monitoring, or attempting to gain access to PrimeCore systems or network traffic.
* Using the PrimeCore network for excessive personal streaming, gaming, cryptocurrency mining, or other high-data activity.
* Connecting unauthorised devices, access points, network equipment, or storage devices to PrimeCore infrastructure.

## 9. Monitoring and Privacy

Casual staff should not expect personal use of PrimeCore accounts, networks, or services to be private. PrimeCore may monitor, log, filter, audit, or investigate access to its systems where reasonably required for security, legal compliance, incident response, or policy enforcement.

PrimeCore will not take ownership of a casual staff member’s personal device. However, PrimeCore may remove its accounts, files, access tokens, or managed work data from the device when access is no longer required.

## 10. Reporting Security Issues

Casual staff must report suspected security issues as soon as possible, including:

* Accidental access to files or folders not intended for them.
* Suspicious emails, links, attachments, or login requests.
* A lost or stolen device that has accessed PrimeCore systems.
* Suspected malware or unusual device behaviour.
* Accidental sharing, downloading, or disclosure of PrimeCore information.

Reports should be made to the staff member’s PrimeCore supervisor or the IT/security contact.

## 11. Breaches and Access Removal

PrimeCore may immediately suspend or remove BYOD and network access if this policy is breached or if a security risk is identified.

Serious or repeated breaches may result in the end of the casual engagement, reporting to relevant authorities where appropriate, or other action consistent with applicable law and PrimeCore procedures.

By connecting a personal device to PrimeCore systems or accepting access to PrimeCore files, casual staff acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to follow this policy.
