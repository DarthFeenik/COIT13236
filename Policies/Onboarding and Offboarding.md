# PrimeCore Minerals  
## IT Device Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure

### 1. Purpose

This procedure explains how PrimeCore Minerals issues, manages, returns, and securely removes access from company laptops, mobile phones, and related equipment.

It applies to permanent staff issued with PrimeCore devices and supports the company’s acceptable-use, security, and information-handling policies.

### 2. Device Onboarding

Before a new employee starts, the hiring manager must advise IT of the employee’s name, role, start date, manager, required access, and equipment needs.

IT must prepare and record each device before issue, including:

- Laptop and mobile phone make, model, serial number, asset tag, and condition.
- Charger, dock, headset, SIM/eSIM, protective case, and other supplied accessories.
- Assigned mobile number and data plan, where applicable.
- Employee name, department, issue date, and expected return date if known.

Before issue, IT must ensure the device:

- Has a supported operating system and current security updates.
- Has full-disk encryption, password/PIN protection, screen lock, and approved anti-malware or endpoint protection enabled.
- Is enrolled in PrimeCore’s approved device-management system.
- Has only approved business applications, accounts, and security settings installed.
- Has access limited to the systems, files, and network services needed for the employee’s role.
- Has a current backup and recovery method configured where required.

### 3. Employee Device Handover

When equipment is issued, the employee must:

- Inspect the device and accessories and report any visible damage immediately.
- Set a unique password or PIN and complete multi-factor authentication where required.
- Read and acknowledge the PrimeCore IT Acceptable Use and Device Policy.
- Keep the laptop, phone, SIM/eSIM, and accessories secure and for their own use only.
- Not share passwords, PINs, authentication codes, or devices with others.
- Promptly report loss, theft, damage, suspicious activity, or accidental exposure of information.
- Use approved remote-access methods when working away from a PrimeCore location.
- Return all issued equipment when requested or when employment ends.

The employee must sign an asset acknowledgement confirming the equipment received and their responsibility for its reasonable care and return.

### 4. Changes During Employment

Where an employee changes role, location, manager, or employment status, their system and device access must be reviewed by their manager and IT.

IT must remove access that is no longer required and provide only the additional access needed for the new role. Old devices must be returned before replacement equipment is issued unless approved by IT.

### 5. Offboarding Notification

The employee’s manager or Human Resources must notify IT as soon as an employee’s departure is confirmed. The notification must include:

- Employee name and role.
- Final working day and expected final system-access time.
- Whether the departure is planned, immediate, or high risk.
- A list of known company devices, accounts, access cards, keys, and other equipment issued.

For immediate or high-risk departures, IT may remove access before or at the direction of Human Resources or management.

### 6. Access Removal

By the employee’s final working day, or earlier where directed, IT must:

- Disable or remove access to PrimeCore accounts, email, cloud services, remote access, VPN, collaboration systems, and internal applications.
- Revoke multi-factor authentication methods, sessions, device certificates, and access tokens.
- Remove the employee from shared drives, file-sharing groups, project spaces, and distribution lists.
- Transfer ownership of required business files, email, and records to the employee’s manager or nominated staff member.
- Update asset and access records to show that offboarding is in progress or completed.

Access removal must be recorded for audit purposes.

### 7. Return of Equipment

Employees must return all PrimeCore property no later than their final working day, unless another arrangement has been approved in writing.

This includes:

- Laptop, mobile phone, charger, dock, headset, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and protective case.
- SIM/eSIM, security token, access card, keys, and storage devices.
- Any printed documents, notebooks, or other material containing PrimeCore information.

Equipment must be returned directly to IT, the employee’s manager, or another approved PrimeCore representative. Returned devices must not be left unattended or passed through an unauthorised person.

### 8. Device Inspection and Data Handling

When equipment is returned, IT must:

- Check the asset tag, serial number, accessories, physical condition, and any reported damage.
- Confirm that business information has been retained or transferred where required.
- Remove PrimeCore accounts and credentials from the device.
- Securely erase or rebuild the device using approved processes before it is reassigned, repaired, sold, or disposed of.
- Update the asset register to show the device’s returned condition and next status.
- Escalate missing, damaged, or unreturned equipment to the employee’s manager and Human Resources.

PrimeCore information must not be copied to an employee’s personal device, email account, cloud storage, or removable media before departure.

### 9. Unreturned, Lost, or Stolen Devices

If a device is not returned, is lost, or is stolen, the employee must notify PrimeCore immediately.

IT may remotely lock, locate, or erase a company device where supported and appropriate. PrimeCore may investigate the incident and take further action in line with employment agreements, workplace policies, and applicable law.

### 10. Records and Review

IT must retain onboarding and offboarding records, including asset acknowledgements, access-removal records, return checklists, and device-wipe confirmation.

This procedure should be reviewed at least annually or after a significant security incident, technology change, or change to PrimeCore’s working arrangements.
